Hotel Management System

An academic project focused on developing a hotel management system to organize reservations and simplify hotel operations.

Project Overview

The system helps customers check room availability, select rooms, and make reservations. It also allows receptionists to manage bookings and hotel managers to monitor hotel information and financial reports.

Main Features

  • Room availability and booking management.
  • Customer reservation information.
  • Updating and modifying booking details.
  • Managing room categories and prices.
  • Generating financial reports.

Project Goals

To make hotel operations faster, easier, more organized, and more secure.
